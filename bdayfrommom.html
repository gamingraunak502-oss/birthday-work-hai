Mere sabse pyaare bete, ❤️

Jis din tum meri zindagi mein aaye the, us din meri duniya hi badal gayi thi. Tumhari pehli muskurahat se lekar aaj tak, tum meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat khushi ho. 🥹

Shayad main har baar apne dil ki baat lafzon mein na keh paaun, lekin beta, tum mere liye duniya ki har khushi se badhkar ho. Tumhari muskurahat meri khushi hai aur tumhare aansu mere dil ko dard dete hain. ❤️

Meri dua hai ki tum zindagi mein khoob aage badho, tumhare saare sapne poore hon aur tumhe har kadam par khushiyan milen. Bhagwan tumhe hamesha sehatmand, khush aur salamat rakhe. 🤲

Zindagi mein kabhi mushkil waqt aaye, toh yaad rakhna beta, tumhari Mumma ka pyaar hamesha tumhare saath hai. Duniya chahe tumhara saath de ya na de, meri duaayein aur mera pyaar kabhi tumhara saath nahi chhodenge. 🫂❤️

Tum chahe kitne bhi bade ho jao, meri nazron mein hamesha mere nanhe se bachche hi rahoge. Tum meri jaan ho, meri khushi ho aur meri duniya ho. ❤️

Bhagwan tumhe har buri nazar se bachaye, meri jaan. 🤲❤️
